OUR FOCUS
Two crises, one mission.
Drug abuse & addiction
We expose the dangers of substance use disorders, rescue those caught in addiction, and walk them through detox, rehab, and re-entry into life.
Sex trafficking
We rescue victims of trafficking and provide safe shelter, counseling, re-orientation, and the training needed to reclaim a future.
OUR INTERVENTION PATHWAY
From rescue to a rebuilt life — six structured steps.
No one is dropped between stages. Each step has trained staff and a clear outcome.
1
Detox
Medical withdrawal under care.
2
Rehab
Counseling and therapy.
3
Shelter
Safe transitional housing.
4
Re-orientation
Values and life skills.
5
Training
Vocational skill-building.
6
Empowerment
Job placement, alumni support.

WHO WE ARE
A registered Nigerian foundation, built around dignity.
Gateway to Better Life Foundation is led by faith leaders, professionals, and survivors who believe rescue is just the first step — restoration is the goal. We're registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission of Nigeria and accountable to the communities we serve.
